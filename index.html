About Hislerim

Varanasi's premier dining destination where culinary artistry meets warm hospitality.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for exceptional cuisine, Hislerim brings together the flavors of the world in the spiritual heart of India. Our name means "My Feelings" in Turkish, reflecting our emotional connection to food and hospitality.

Culinary Excellence

Experience a diverse menu featuring North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Mexican, and Mediterranean cuisines. Every dish is crafted with premium ingredients and authentic recipes.

Perfect Ambience

Immerse yourself in our aesthetic woody décor complemented by soothing music. The warm, inviting atmosphere makes Hislerim ideal for intimate dinners, family gatherings, and corporate events.

Global Culinary Journey

Experience a world of finely-tuned flavors under one roof, crafted to satisfy local cravings and international travelers alike.

Authentic Flavors from Around the World

At Hislerim, we pride ourselves on offering authentic dishes that transport you across culinary borders. Every meal is carefully curated and prepared by specialized chefs using traditional recipes, fresh local produce, and premium imported herbs to deliver an uncompromised fine-dining experience.

Indo-Chinese & Handcrafted Dim Sums

Crispy wok-tossed spring rolls, savory Hakka noodles, and delicate, steam-cooked dim sums featuring gourmet corn, cheese, and exotic vegetable fillings.

Gourmet Continental & Premium Fast Food

Crispy hand-stretched pizzas, loaded cheese grilled sandwiches, thick premium shakes, and golden french fries served with culinary dips.

Varanasi Classics & Sizzling Desserts

Experience local culinary tradition with sweet, creamy Banarasi Lassi, fragrant baseline biryanis, and our hot sizzling brownie desserts.

Experience Our Ambience

Immerse yourself in our warm woody atmosphere, thoughtfully crafted with premium textures and soothing acoustics.

A Feast for All Senses

Curated Music

Our playlist is soft and instrumental, selected specifically to create the perfect background for meaningful conversations without overpowering your dining experience.

Thoughtful Warm Lighting

Mellow, dynamic lighting sets an intimate tone. We avoid harsh glares to offer a comforting workspace by day and a cozy celebratory space by night.

Aesthetic Woody Decor

Beautifully carved paneling, elegant custom tables, and soft earthly elements capture a rustic yet highly sophisticated interior environment.

Versatile Seating Setups

Whether you need quiet romantic date, spacious center tables for family dinners, or dynamic setups for corporate board meetups, we configure the layout to your comfort.
